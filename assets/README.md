# Presentation assets

The ETF emblem is sourced from the cover of the School of Electrical Engineering's official [faculty laboratory document (February 2025)](https://www.etf.bg.ac.rs/uploads/files/obavestenja/Samovrednovanje/2024-25/Prilog_11.4_Spisak_laboratorija_2024_2025_sa_opisima.pdf). The original 611 × 688 PNG is retained as `etf-logo.png` and used to identify the University of Belgrade, School of Electrical Engineering.

`research-banner.svg` is the editable, self-contained source; `research-banner.png` is the README export.
